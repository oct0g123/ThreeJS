🌿 Little Garden

🌿 Welcome to Little Garden

A tiny floating island that's all yours. Dig rivers, plant trees, and build a sleepy village.

  • Pick something from the bar at the bottom, then click to place it. Flowers, bushes and lily pads can be dragged to scatter. Fences draw in lines.
  • Land tools sculpt hills, carve rivers and paint paths. Hold Shift to reverse a brush.
  • Right-drag (or two fingers) to spin the camera, scroll to zoom and middle-drag or WASD to pan.
  • R rotates, [ ] resize the brush, Ctrl+Z undoes, P is photo mode.
  • Slide the ☀️ to make it night and watch the lanterns come on.

Your garden saves itself in this browser.
